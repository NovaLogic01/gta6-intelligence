# ASO STRATEGY (App Store Optimization)

*Note: This platform is currently a web property. This document outlines the App Store Readiness package for future PWA or native distribution, following strict guidelines without claiming an app currently exists.*

## 1. STORE LISTING ASSETS (CONCEPT)
- **App Name Candidate:** `GTA VI Intelligence Platform`
- **Short Description:** `Verified GTA 6 database and timeline.`
- **Long Description:** `The GTA VI Intelligence Platform is a strict, source-verified database tracking characters, locations, vehicles, and timeline events for the next generation of Grand Theft Auto. Review official signals, track changes, and explore the complete intelligence graph.`
- **Icon Direction:** Dark charcoal background with a high-contrast electric blue geometric monogram/crosshair. Avoid any Rockstar-copyrighted assets.
- **Feature Graphic:** Cinematic topographical map with coordinate overlays, showcasing the "Database Online" aesthetic.

## 2. SCREENSHOT SEQUENCE
1. **The Graph:** Abstract representation of the knowledge graph linking characters and locations.
2. **The Database:** A structured dossier of a verified character.
3. **The Timeline:** The interactive chronology interface.
4. **The Source:** Demonstration of strict source attribution (Official vs Reported).
5. **The Map:** The terminal-style topographic map interface.

## 3. KEYWORD STRATEGY
- **Primary:** `GTA 6`, `GTA VI`, `GTA 6 news`, `GTA VI map`
- **Secondary:** `Leonida`, `Vice City map`, `GTA 6 characters`
- **Constraint:** Do not use trademarked names in a deceptive manner. App must be clearly presented as an independent database/intelligence tracker.

## 4. LOCALIZATION PLAN
- Initial focus: English (US, UK).
- Future phase: Spanish (LatAm) and Portuguese (Brazil) due to high regional franchise interest, ensuring manual, high-quality translation of the intelligence graph.
