# ORGANIC GROWTH SYSTEM

## CORE PHILOSOPHY
Growth for the GTA VI Intelligence Platform is driven by establishing authority and trust. We do not use spam, scrape competing publishers, fabricate trending stories, or engage in fake engagement metrics.

## GROWTH PILLARS

### 1. Evergreen Explainer Hubs
Create comprehensive, deeply researched pages on core topics (e.g., "The History of Vice City vs. Leonida"). These capture high-intent search traffic looking for lore and verified background information, rather than fleeting rumors.

### 2. Database Discoverability
Each entity (Character, Vehicle, Location, Feature) in the intelligence database has a dedicated, statically generated URL with rich Schema.org metadata. By maintaining high factual density on these pages, we capture long-tail search queries (e.g., "GTA VI Declasse Tulip confirmed").

### 3. Timeline Intelligence Sharing
The chronological timeline of GTA VI's development is highly shareable. We will promote major timeline milestones on social platforms, linking directly back to the timeline UI for full context.

### 4. Verified News Filtering
When major rumors break, our platform provides a "Status Check" article. We evaluate the rumor against our methodology and assign it a `RUMOR` or `REPORTED` tag. This creates a trusted destination for players trying to verify if a viral social media post is real or fake.

### 5. Short-Form Video Funnel (YouTube Shorts / TikTok)
Convert our database intelligence into quick, 60-second factual breakdowns.
- *Format:* "3 Confirmed Vehicles in GTA VI" or "What we actually know about Jason."
- *Call to Action:* "Check the full intelligence report and sources at GTA6 Intel."
- *Funnel:* Direct traffic to specific `/database` or `/news` routes.

### 6. Semantic Internal Linking
Ensure every article and entity naturally links to related database nodes. This creates a web of intelligence that keeps users on the platform longer (increasing session duration) and helps search engine crawlers understand topical authority.
